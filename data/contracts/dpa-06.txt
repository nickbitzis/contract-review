DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

between Halden Health Partners ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Supplier")

1) Definitions
1.1) "Agreement" means this agreement between Supplier and Halden Health Partners, including all Order Forms.
1.2) "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
1.3) "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
1.4) "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

2) Subprocessors
Halden Health Partners has the right to veto a new subprocessor during a thirty (30) day review window.

3) Audit Rights
Halden Health Partners may review Supplier's compliance records one time every twelve months.

4) Security Incidents
Supplier shall notify Halden Health Partners of any Security Incident within twelve (12) hours of becoming aware of it.

5) Liability
Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set forth in the Agreement.

6) Confidentiality
6.1) Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
6.2) The duty of secrecy lasts two years beyond expiry.

7) Scope and Roles
Halden Health Partners is the controller and Supplier is the processor of Halden Health Partners Personal Data processed under the Agreement.

8) Processing Instructions
Supplier shall process Halden Health Partners Personal Data only on Halden Health Partners' documented instructions.

9) Return and Deletion
Within thirty (30) days after termination, Supplier shall delete or return all Halden Health Partners Personal Data.
