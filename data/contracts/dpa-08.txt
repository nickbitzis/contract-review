Data Processing Addendum

between Stillwater Media ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Vendor")

Section 1. Definitions
(a) "Agreement" means this agreement between Vendor and Stillwater Media, including all Order Forms.
(b) "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
(c) "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
(d) "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

Section 2. Scope and Roles
Stillwater Media is the controller and Vendor is the processor of Stillwater Media Personal Data processed under the Agreement.

Section 3. Processing Instructions
Vendor shall process Stillwater Media Personal Data only on Stillwater Media's documented instructions.

Section 4. Subprocessors
Vendor will notify Stillwater Media at least thirty (30) days before engaging a new subprocessor, and Stillwater Media may object on reasonable data protection grounds.

Section 5. Breach Notification
Vendor shall notify Stillwater Media of any Security Incident within one (1) business day of becoming aware of it.

Section 6. Liability
Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set forth in the Agreement.

Section 7. Return and Deletion
Within thirty (30) days after termination, Vendor shall delete or return all Stillwater Media Personal Data.

Section 8. Compliance Verification
Vendor will provide its most recent SOC 2 Type II report on request, no more than once per year.
