Data Processing Addendum

between Ashby Capital ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Vendor")

1. Definitions
1.1 "Agreement" means this agreement between Vendor and Ashby Capital, including all Order Forms.
1.2 "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
1.3 "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
1.4 "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

2. Return and Deletion
Within thirty (30) days after termination, Vendor shall delete or return all Ashby Capital Personal Data.

3. Non-Disclosure
3.1 Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
3.2 These obligations continue indefinitely.

5. Compliance Verification
Vendor will provide its most recent SOC 2 Type II report on request, no more than once per year.

7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in San Francisco County, California have exclusive jurisdiction.

8. Scope and Roles
Ashby Capital is the controller and Vendor is the processor of Ashby Capital Personal Data processed under the Agreement.

9. Security Incidents
Notification of a Security Incident is due no later than forty-eight hours after Vendor learns of it.

10. Sub-processing
Ashby Capital has the right to veto a new subprocessor during a thirty (30) day review window.

11. Processing Instructions
Vendor shall process Ashby Capital Personal Data only on Ashby Capital's documented instructions.
