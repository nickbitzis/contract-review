Master Subscription Agreement

between Thorne Insurance ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Supplier")

DEFINITIONS
"Agreement" means this agreement between Supplier and Thorne Insurance, including all Order Forms.
"Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
"Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
"Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except for the Excluded Claims, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Thorne Insurance in the thirty-six (36) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits, even if advised of their possibility.
The limitations in this Section do not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations, (b) breach of confidentiality obligations, or (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct.

PERFORMANCE WARRANTY
Supplier warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation for ninety (90) days after the start of the Subscription Term.
Supplier further warrants that the Services are fit for Thorne Insurance's intended purpose.

FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control. If such an event lasts more than thirty (30) days, either party may end the affected Order Form.

DURATION
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms under it have expired or been terminated.
This Agreement automatically renews for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of written notice.
Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.
Upon termination, Thorne Insurance shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other party's Confidential Information.

CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
These obligations continue indefinitely.

NOTICES
Notices must be in writing and are deemed received two (2) days after mailing or on delivery by email.

INDEMNIFICATION
Supplier shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Thorne Insurance and its affiliates, officers, and employees from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, and expenses arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, and shall pay all damages and costs finally awarded.
The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, sole control of the defense, and reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

TRANSFER
Thorne Insurance may transfer this Agreement to any affiliate or successor. Supplier may not transfer it without Thorne Insurance's approval.

SERVICES
Supplier will make the Services available to Thorne Insurance during the Subscription Term in accordance with the Documentation.

FEES AND PAYMENT
Supplier will invoice Thorne Insurance annually in advance for the fees in each Order Form.
Thorne Insurance shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.
Fees exclude taxes. Each party is responsible for the taxes imposed on it by law.

MISCELLANEOUS
This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter.
The parties are independent contractors.
