Master Subscription Agreement

between Morrow Hospitality ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Vendor")

1. Definitions. "Agreement" means this agreement between Vendor and Morrow Hospitality, including all Order Forms.
1.2. "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
1.3. "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
1.4. "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

2. Maximum Recovery. Neither party's aggregate exposure for claims connected with this Agreement will be greater than the amounts paid by Morrow Hospitality during the twenty-four (24) month period immediately before the claim.
2.2. Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits, even if advised of their possibility.

3. Notices. Notices must be in writing and are deemed received two (2) days after mailing or on delivery by email.

4. Excluded Claims. The limitations in Section 2 do not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations, (b) breach of confidentiality obligations, or (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct.

5. Support and Service Levels. Vendor will respond to Severity 1 issues within four (4) hours and to Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day.

6. Insurance. Vendor will carry cyber coverage of at least 1 million dollars per incident.

7. Non-Solicitation. Neither party will recruit the other's personnel for eighteen months after the last Order Form ends.

8. Export Compliance. Each party shall comply with applicable export control and sanctions laws.

9. Assignment. Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

10. Third-Party Claims. Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Morrow Hospitality and its affiliates, officers, and employees from and against any third-party claim alleging that the Services infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret, and shall pay all damages and costs finally awarded.
10.2. The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, sole control of the defense, and reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

11. Publicity. Vendor may name Morrow Hospitality as a customer with Morrow Hospitality's prior written approval.

12. Payment. Vendor will invoice Morrow Hospitality annually in advance for the fees in each Order Form.
12.2. Payment terms are net 60 days from the invoice date.
12.3. Unpaid balances carry a monthly finance charge of 1 percent.
12.4. Fees exclude taxes. Each party is responsible for the taxes imposed on it by law.

13. Data Protection. Vendor shall process Morrow Hospitality Personal Data only on Morrow Hospitality's documented instructions.
13.2. Vendor shall notify Morrow Hospitality of any Security Incident within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.
13.3. Vendor shall not engage any subprocessor without Morrow Hospitality's prior written consent.
13.4. Morrow Hospitality may review Vendor's compliance records one time every twelve months.

14. Disputes. Any dispute will be resolved under Delaware law, and the parties consent to venue in Wilmington, Delaware.

15. Services. Vendor will make the Services available to Morrow Hospitality during the Subscription Term in accordance with the Documentation.

16. Confidential Information. Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
16.2. The duty of secrecy lasts five years beyond expiry.

17. Term and Termination. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms under it have expired or been terminated.
17.2. Each Subscription Term will renew for an additional twelve (12) months unless a party notifies the other of its intent not to renew no later than 30 days prior to expiration.
17.3. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of written notice.
17.4. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.
17.5. Upon termination, Morrow Hospitality shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other party's Confidential Information.

18. Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control. If such an event lasts more than thirty (30) days, either party may end the affected Order Form.

19. Performance Warranty. Vendor warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation for three (3) months after the start of the Subscription Term.
19.2. Vendor further warrants that the Services are fit for Morrow Hospitality's intended purpose.

20. Miscellaneous. This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter.
20.2. The parties are independent contractors.
