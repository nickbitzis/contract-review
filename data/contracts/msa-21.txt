SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

between Wexley Media ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Vendor")

ARTICLE I - DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Agreement" means this agreement between Vendor and Wexley Media, including all Order Forms.
1.2 "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
1.3 "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
1.4 "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

ARTICLE II - DISPUTES
2.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in San Francisco County, California have exclusive jurisdiction.

ARTICLE III - NO HIRE
3.1 Neither party will recruit the other's personnel for eighteen months after the last Order Form ends.

ARTICLE IV - INDEMNIFICATION
4.1 Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Wexley Media and its affiliates, officers, and employees from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, and expenses arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, and shall pay all damages and costs finally awarded.
4.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, sole control of the defense, and reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

ARTICLE V - CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
5.1 Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
5.2 These obligations continue indefinitely.

ARTICLE VI - EXPORT COMPLIANCE
6.1 Each party shall comply with applicable export control and sanctions laws.

ARTICLE VII - NOTICES
7.1 Notices must be in writing and are deemed received two (2) days after mailing or on delivery by email.

ARTICLE VIII - REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
8.1 Vendor warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation for three (3) months after the start of the Subscription Term.
8.2 Vendor further warrants that the Services are fit for Wexley Media's intended purpose.

ARTICLE IX - INVOICING AND PAYMENT
9.1 Vendor will invoice Wexley Media annually in advance for the fees in each Order Form.
9.2 Wexley Media shall pay all undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt.
9.3 Late payments bear interest at 0.5% per month.
9.4 Fees exclude taxes. Each party is responsible for the taxes imposed on it by law.

ARTICLE X - DURATION
10.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms under it have expired or been terminated.
10.2 This Agreement automatically renews for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.
10.3 If one party seriously fails to perform and has not corrected the failure within 30 days after being told about it, the other party may end this Agreement.
10.4 Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.
10.5 Upon termination, Wexley Media shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other party's Confidential Information.

ARTICLE XI - LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
11.1 In no event will either party's total liability under this Agreement exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the annual fees payable under this Agreement.
11.2 Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits, even if advised of their possibility.
11.3 The limitations in this Section do not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations, (b) breach of confidentiality obligations, or (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (d) Vendor's breach of its data protection or security obligations.

ARTICLE XII - PUBLICITY
12.1 Vendor may name Wexley Media as a customer with Wexley Media's prior written approval.

ARTICLE XIII - ASSIGNMENT
13.1 Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

ARTICLE XIV - MISCELLANEOUS
14.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter.
14.2 The parties are independent contractors.
