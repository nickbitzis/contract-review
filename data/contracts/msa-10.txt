Master Subscription Agreement

between Dunmore Capital ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Provider")

1. Definitions. "Agreement" means this agreement between Provider and Dunmore Capital, including all Order Forms.
1.2. "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
1.3. "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
1.4. "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

2. Support and Service Levels. Provider will respond to Severity 1 issues within four (4) hours and to Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day.

3. Payment. Provider will invoice Dunmore Capital annually in advance for the fees in each Order Form.
3.2. Amounts invoiced fall due sixty days from the invoice date.
3.3. Overdue amounts accrue interest at the lesser of 1% per month or the highest rate permitted by law.
3.4. Fees exclude taxes. Each party is responsible for the taxes imposed on it by law.

4. Export Compliance. Each party shall comply with applicable export control and sanctions laws.

5. Choice of Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois have exclusive jurisdiction.

6. Data Protection. Provider shall process Dunmore Capital Personal Data only on Dunmore Capital's documented instructions.
6.2. Notification of a Security Incident is due no later than forty-eight hours after Provider learns of it.
6.3. Dunmore Capital authorizes Provider to engage subprocessors. Provider will maintain a list of subprocessors and notify Dunmore Capital of any additions.
6.4. Provider will provide its most recent SOC 2 Type II report on request, no more than once per year.

7. Services. Provider will make the Services available to Dunmore Capital during the Subscription Term in accordance with the Documentation.

8. Indemnification. Each party (the "Indemnifying Party") shall defend and hold harmless the other party from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, and expenses arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, and shall pay all damages and costs finally awarded.
8.2. The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, sole control of the defense, and reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

9. Warranties. Provider warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation for three (3) months after the start of the Subscription Term.
9.2. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY PROVIDED IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

10. Liability. The parties agree that no limitation of liability applies to Provider's obligations under this Agreement, and Provider's liability is uncapped.

11. Notices. Notices must be in writing and are deemed received two (2) days after mailing or on delivery by email.

12. Transfer. No party may transfer its rights or duties under this Agreement without written approval, though either party may do so to an entity that acquires its business.

13. Publicity. Provider may name Dunmore Capital as a customer with Dunmore Capital's prior written approval.

14. Automatic Renewal. Unless cancelled in writing 60 days ahead of the anniversary date, the subscription rolls over for another year.

15. Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control. If such an event lasts more than thirty (30) days, either party may end the affected Order Form.

16. Confidentiality. Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
16.2. These obligations apply for a period of 5 years from the date of disclosure.

17. Duration. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms under it have expired or been terminated.
17.2. If one party seriously fails to perform and has not corrected the failure within 30 days after being told about it, the other party may end this Agreement.
17.3. Upon termination, Dunmore Capital shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other party's Confidential Information.

18. Miscellaneous. This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter.
18.2. The parties are independent contractors.
