SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

between Wexley Health Partners ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Provider")

Section 1. Definitions
(a) "Agreement" means this agreement between Provider and Wexley Health Partners, including all Order Forms.
(b) "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
(c) "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
(d) "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

Section 2. Warranties
Provider warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation for three (3) months after the start of the Subscription Term.

Section 3. Insurance
Provider shall maintain, at its own expense, commercial general liability and cyber liability insurance with limits of not less than $10,000,000 per occurrence.

Section 4. Transfer
Provider may not assign this Agreement without Wexley Health Partners' prior written consent. Wexley Health Partners may assign this Agreement freely.

Section 5. Disclaimer
EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY PROVIDED IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

Section 6. Duration
(a) This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms under it have expired or been terminated.
(b) This Agreement automatically renews for successive one (1) year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
(c) Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within 30 days of written notice.
(d) Upon termination, Wexley Health Partners shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other party's Confidential Information.

Section 7. Support and Service Levels
Provider will respond to Severity 1 issues within four (4) hours and to Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day.

Section 8. Notices
Notices must be in writing and are deemed received two (2) days after mailing or on delivery by email.

Section 9. Indemnification
(a) The parties will each protect the other against actions brought by outsiders asserting that the Services violate a third party's patent, copyright, or trade secret.
(b) The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, sole control of the defense, and reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

Section 10. Payment
(a) Provider will invoice Wexley Health Partners annually in advance for the fees in each Order Form.
(b) Amounts invoiced fall due ninety days from the invoice date.
(c) Overdue amounts accrue interest at the lesser of 0.5% per month or the highest rate permitted by law.
(d) Fees exclude taxes. Each party is responsible for the taxes imposed on it by law.

Section 11. Confidentiality
(a) Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
(b) The duty of secrecy has no end date.

Section 12. Publicity
Provider may name Wexley Health Partners as a customer with Wexley Health Partners' prior written approval.

Section 13. Disputes
Any dispute will be resolved under Ontario law, and the parties consent to venue in Toronto, Ontario.

Section 14. Non-Solicitation
During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who worked on the Services.

Section 15. Miscellaneous
(a) This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter.
(b) The parties are independent contractors.
