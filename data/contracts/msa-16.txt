SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

between Morrow Media ("Customer") and Tessavel, Inc. ("Provider")

1. Definitions. "Agreement" means this agreement between Provider and Morrow Media, including all Order Forms.
1.2. "Fees" means the amounts payable under an Order Form.
1.3. "Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data in the Services.
1.4. "Subscription Term" means the period stated in an Order Form.

2. Publicity. Provider may name Morrow Media as a customer with Morrow Media's prior written approval.

3. Termination. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms under it have expired or been terminated.
3.2. Each Subscription Term will renew for an additional twelve (12) months unless a party notifies the other of its intent not to renew no later than 90 days prior to expiration.
3.3. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving notice of it.
3.4. Upon termination, Morrow Media shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other party's Confidential Information.

4. Export Compliance. Each party shall comply with applicable export control and sanctions laws.

5. Support and Service Levels. Provider will respond to Severity 1 issues within four (4) hours and to Severity 2 issues within one (1) business day.

6. Notices. Notices must be in writing and are deemed received two (2) days after mailing or on delivery by email.

7. Limitation of Liability. Liability of each party is restricted to 200% of the Fees for the then-current year.
7.2. Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits, even if advised of their possibility.

8. Data Protection. Provider shall process Morrow Media Personal Data only on Morrow Media's documented instructions.
8.2. Notification of a Security Incident is due no later than forty-eight hours after Provider learns of it.
8.3. Provider will notify Morrow Media at least thirty (30) days before engaging a new subprocessor, and Morrow Media may object on reasonable data protection grounds.
8.4. Morrow Media may audit Provider's compliance once per year on thirty (30) days' prior notice, by remote document review.

9. Indemnification. Each party (the "Indemnifying Party") shall defend and hold harmless the other party from and against any third-party claim alleging that the Services infringe or misappropriate any intellectual property right, or arising from a breach of the data protection obligations in this Agreement, and shall pay all damages and costs finally awarded.
9.2. The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, sole control of the defense, and reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

10. Confidentiality. Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other. The receiving party shall use it only to perform this Agreement and shall protect it with at least reasonable care.
10.2. The duty of secrecy lasts seven years beyond expiry.

11. Invoicing and Payment. Provider will invoice Morrow Media annually in advance for the fees in each Order Form.
11.2. Morrow Media shall pay all undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt.
11.3. Unpaid balances carry a monthly finance charge of 1.5 percent.
11.4. Fees exclude taxes. Each party is responsible for the taxes imposed on it by law.

12. Excluded Claims. The limitations in Section 7 do not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations, (b) breach of confidentiality obligations, or (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct.

13. Transfer. No party may transfer its rights or duties under this Agreement without written approval, though either party may do so to an entity that acquires its business.

14. Performance Warranty. Provider stands behind the Services for three months: if they do not work as documented, Provider will fix them.
14.2. Provider further warrants that the Services are fit for Morrow Media's intended purpose.

15. Miscellaneous. This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter.
15.2. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The state and federal courts located in New York County, New York have exclusive jurisdiction.
15.3. The parties are independent contractors.
